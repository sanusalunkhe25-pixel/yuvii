For you to read whenever you need a little reminder. ♡

Dear Yuvii, ♡

Happy Birthday, my favourite person. I hope this new chapter brings you happiness, peace, good health, success and beautiful opportunities.

I want you to know that I appreciate you for who you are. It's not only about the big moments. Sometimes, it's the little conversations, the laughter and the simple memories that become the most meaningful.

I hope you never stop believing in yourself. Keep working towards your dreams, take care of yourself and give yourself credit for how far you've come.

And for us, I hope we continue to grow in a way that feels healthy and genuine. I wish for more understanding, honest conversations, mutual respect, consistency and effort from both of us.

We don't need to be perfect. I hope we keep learning from our mistakes, listening to each other and making space for each other's feelings and dreams.

Today, I simply wish you a beautiful year ahead. May you have more reasons to smile, more goals to achieve and more moments that make you proud of yourself.

Happy Birthday once again, Yuvii. You deserve the best. ♡

With love, always ♡
